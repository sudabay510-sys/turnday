# Turnday — Brand identity

**Slug:** `turnday` · **Tagline:** A turnover-and-messaging playbook for independent short-term rental hosts.
**Status:** Early access. Checkout not live; founding list only. No payments processed.

## Positioning
Independent hosts and co-hosts running 1–10 listings without a full property-management system.

**Promise (honest):** Operations checklists only. No occupancy, revenue, rating, or Superhost guarantees.

## Personas
- **Side-gig host:** One listing, a day job, and turnovers that eat Sunday.
- **Growing host:** 2–5 listings where every process now lives in your head.
- **Co-host:** You run someone else’s place and need repeatable standards.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Booking** — pain: Guests ask the same five questions. → plan: Saved replies and a confirmation message in your tone.
2. **Pre-arrival** — pain: Check-in confusion and late-night calls. → plan: Numbered entry steps with one photo each.
3. **Stay** — pain: Small problems become bad reviews. → plan: A morning-after check-in message.
4. **Turnover** — pain: Rushed cleans and missing supplies. → plan: Room-by-room checklist and restock pars.

## Voice & tone
Calm, practical, hospitable. Like an experienced co-host leaving you a tidy note.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing (planned terms; not live)
- **4-week reset** — $19 first 4 weeks, then $24 / 4 weeks unless cancelled
- **12-week season** — $49 first 12 weeks, then $59 / 12 weeks unless cancelled

## Do
- Write like a host who has done 500 turnovers.
- Mention the platform’s own tools when relevant.
- Show intro **and** renewal prices before any future charge.
- Label the founding list as “no charge today”.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Imply affiliation with Airbnb or Vrbo.
- Promise bookings, nightly-rate gains, or Superhost.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
